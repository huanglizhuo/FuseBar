# Animation reference

The v2 promotional animation adapts the inverse-radius bending stroke, orbit/growth choreography and staggered dot flight from [CircleStatusBar](https://github.com/artemnovichkov/CircleStatusBar), by Artem Novichkov. These adaptations are in `compositions/frames/01-fusion.html`. The menu-bar zoom, sound-glyph decomposition, composition and product feature animation are custom. No reference video frames are included. The native FuseBar app implementation remains independent.
